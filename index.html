How to Play

On foot

  • W A S D / Arrows — move
  • Shift — sprint
  • Space — jump
  • Mouse — camera (click to lock)
  • Wheel — zoom
  • E — interact / enter & exit vehicles / start missions
  • 1 / 2 / 3 — use Coffee / Nitro / Repair Kit
  • I or Tab — inventory & stats
  • Esc / P — pause

In a vehicle

  • W / S — accelerate / brake & reverse
  • A / D — steer
  • Mouse — chase camera, wheel zoom
  • E — exit vehicle / repair at garages ($200)

Goal

  • Collect cash orbs and items around the city
  • Start missions at the cyan markers (5 missions)
  • Garages repair your car · weather & night affect driving

Touch

  • Left stick — move/steer · right side drag — camera · on-screen buttons
